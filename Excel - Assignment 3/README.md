# Adecco HR Analytics – Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

The Adecco HR Analytics project focuses on analyzing employee data to identify workforce trends, understand factors influencing employee attrition, and provide actionable recommendations for improving employee retention and organizational performance. The analysis evaluates employee demographics, compensation, job satisfaction, work-life balance, training effectiveness, promotion opportunities, and other workforce-related factors using Microsoft Excel.

## Objective

* Identify key factors contributing to employee attrition.
* Analyze employee engagement and satisfaction levels.
* Evaluate the impact of compensation, promotions, and training on retention.
* Understand factors affecting employee performance.
* Provide data-driven recommendations for workforce planning and organizational effectiveness.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* COUNTIF Function
* AVERAGE Function
* Correlation Analysis
* Scatter Plot Analysis
* Trendline Analysis
* Data Visualization Techniques (Bar Charts, Pie Charts)

## Process

1. Performed data cleaning, validation, and missing value handling.
2. Conducted exploratory data analysis on workforce demographics, attrition, compensation, and satisfaction.
3. Performed statistical analysis including correlation analysis, trend analysis, and attrition rate calculations.
4. Created visualizations using Pivot Charts, Bar Charts, Pie Charts, Scatter Plots, and Trendlines.
5. Extracted workforce insights and developed HR recommendations for employee retention and performance improvement.

## Key Insights

* Employee attrition is influenced by compensation, engagement, career growth opportunities, and workplace satisfaction.
* Employees with higher job involvement are significantly less likely to leave the organization.
* Lower monthly income is associated with higher attrition rates.
* Employees living farther from the workplace show higher turnover tendencies.
* Training and stock option benefits contribute positively to employee retention.
* Work-life balance and career development opportunities play a critical role in workforce stability.

## Business Recommendations

* Strengthen employee engagement and recognition programs.
* Improve career development and promotion opportunities.
* Enhance compensation and incentive strategies.
* Promote work-life balance through flexible work arrangements.
* Expand training and skill development programs.
